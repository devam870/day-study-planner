Google Calendar

Your due dates are visible in the Daymark calendar. Connect Google to sync tasks and calendar events.

One-time setup

  1. In Google Cloud Console, enable the Google Calendar API, configure the OAuth consent screen, and create an OAuth Client ID for a web application.
  2. Add your site origin (for example, http://localhost:5500) to Authorized JavaScript origins. Open Daymark through that local server; Google OAuth does not work from a file:// URL.
  3. Paste the Client ID into google-config.js. Do not add a client secret to this website.

Sync imports events from your primary calendar as dated tasks and publishes dated Daymark tasks as all-day events. Timed events keep their calendar date in Daymark; recurring events are imported as individual occurrences. Tasks without due dates are not sent, and completion status stays in Daymark. Sync checks from 30 days ago through the next year. Google access tokens are kept in memory only.
