Gemini

Tell Gemini what you need to cover and when your exam is. It will suggest dated study sessions you can add to your task list.

Your subject, topics, exam date, and daily study limit are sent to Google Gemini through the local backend. In PowerShell, set $env:GEMINI_API_KEY="YOUR_GEMINI_API_KEY", then run python server.py from the project folder and open http://localhost:8000/. Keep your key private; it stays on the server and is never placed in the browser.
